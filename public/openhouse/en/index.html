A few helpful details.

Does my child need experience?

Tech Toy Design is beginner-friendly, with no experience needed. For help choosing the right workshop for your child, chat with our team on LINE.

Do we need to bring an iPad?

iPads are provided for Digital Arts, or your child can bring their own iPad.

When do we receive the artwork?

Tech toys go home after class. Collect 3D prints the next day. Digital Arts includes a printed copy and the artwork sent by email.

Is this the regular course?

This Open House offers 50-minute workshops for ages 4–10. Arcko also offers long-term, project-based courses built around each child.

Where is Arcko?

Visit us in Sathorn, Bangkok. Use the map below for directions, or ask our team on LINE.
